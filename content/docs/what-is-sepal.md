Sepal is a plant collection manager for botanic gardens, arboreta, nurseries, and
personal collections. It records what you hold, where each plant came from, and
where every plant is now.

Four records carry almost everything in the app. A
[taxon](/docs/glossary/#taxon) is a botanical name, an
[accession](/docs/glossary/#accession) is one arrival of one taxon, a
[material](/docs/glossary/#material) is a quantity of an accession at one place,
and a [location](/docs/glossary/#location) is a place that holds material. Sepal
ships the World Flora Online Plantlist, so most of the names you need already
exist and you search for a name instead of typing it.

If plant collection management is new to you, the glossary's [further
reading](/docs/glossary/#further-reading) points at published guidance on the
practice itself.

To use all four records on one real arrival, work through [Your first
week](/docs/your-first-week/).
