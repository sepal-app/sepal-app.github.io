Hi there! Welcome to the first post of the Sepal blog. Sepal is a new-*ish* plant
collection manager for botanic gardens, arboreta, herbaria and anyone who manages plant
collections. It was built to bring order and joy to your plant collection.

I say new-*ish* because Sepal's story started over twenty years ago. In 2005 I was working
at [Belize Botanic Gardens](https://belizebotanic.com) (BBG) and managing their plant
collections using a spreadsheet that I had to copy to a floppy disk to move between the
computer I worked on and the computer connected to the label printer. It was tedious work.

At that point I had a degree in computer science, a short career in tech and experience
with open-source software. There also weren't a lot of options in the early 2000s for
plant collection management software, especially so for botanic gardens with very limited
budgets. That was when I created Bauble. Over time, and with some funding through the
UBC Botanic Garden, Bauble eventually became a relatively
feature-complete desktop application for managing plant collections. It was free and
open-source and even a few other gardens in the world adopted it for their plant
collection.

Fast forward to about 2011 and I had left BBG, had a new job and a new baby and much less
time to allocate to Bauble. Fortunately, development was taken over by Mario Frasca, and he
eventually forked Bauble into what became [Ghini](https://ghini.github.io/).

I never stopped thinking about Bauble and collection management software and toyed around
with several different ideas over the years. The collection management software landscape
also changed quite a bit since 2011 with more choices in apps and more open data and
online services to help with taxon names, conservation status, etc. Still, there seemed to
be a real gap that wasn't being filled for gardens that needed a low cost, simple to run,
feature-complete, free and open-source plant collection management app. It was from here
that Sepal was born.

## What makes Sepal different?

Sepal is designed to be a cost-effective app for managing your plant collections without
compromises. Aside from what you would expect from any other similar applications, there are
things that make Sepal different:

- It's open source so that your invaluable plant records are never locked into a proprietary
  platform. The code can be found on our [GitHub
  page](https://github.com/sepal-app/sepal).
- It can be self-hosted for free so you don't need to pay someone a monthly subscription to run Sepal.
- It's simple to run and doesn't require a separate database service. Our goal is to
  eventually make Sepal as easy to install as any other application on your computer.
- In addition to managing your plant collection it also helps manager your media collection and integrates
  with third-party services like the IUCN Red List, World Flora Online Plant List, BGCI's PlantSearch,
  etc.

We'll also be launching Sepal Cloud, which will be a cost-effective solution for paying us
to host Sepal for you. More to come about that in our next blog post.

So welcome to Sepal and stay tuned. You're gonna love some of our upcoming features and
the joy that Sepal brings to your plant collection.
