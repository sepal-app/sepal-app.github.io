- You can delete accessions, material, taxa, locations, contacts and collections.
- Accession and material codes can follow a template set in Settings, such as `{year}.{seq:0000}`, and the create form fills in the next code.
- Search finds a plant by its common name, and a taxon's panel lists its vernacular names.
- A quoted phrase in search matches as a phrase, and a word with a leading `-` excludes records that contain it.
- You can record the parents of a hybrid, and the `parentage:` search field finds every taxon with a given parent in its cross.
- The taxon form suggests a rank and a parent from the name you type.
- Pickers are rebuilt so that a screen reader announces each one by its label. Results are ranked by how well they match.
- You can archive a location that is no longer in use. An archived location keeps its history but leaves the pickers.
- A contact can be a person without a business name, and has separate fields for street, second line and city. Nursery, seed bank and arboretum are new contact types.
- The app works on a phone. The navigation drawer closes after you choose a section, and the record panel opens over the page.
- Edits to an accession's collection details appear in the activity log, and the feed groups events by the garden's day instead of by UTC.
- A self-hosted instance can declare that its backups are managed elsewhere, and Settings then shows only the list of downloads.
