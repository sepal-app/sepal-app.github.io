- Sepal is available in Spanish. Choose a language on your profile page, or keep English even when your browser asks for Spanish.
- Observations replace notes on material. An observation records what someone saw and when, with a type and a value that can be counted.
- You can record propagation from a garden's own stock, from the parent accession or a single plant.
- Media thumbnails are resized instead of loading the full upload.
- A location has a Media tab, and every media grid has a Small, Medium and Large size control.
- The material list shows each plant's status and has an **Only living material** checkbox.
- The separator between accession and material codes is a setting, and code templates accept a `{letter}` token for codes such as `2026.0001A`.
- The accession, collection and propagation forms refuse dates in the future.
- Switching tabs on a record no longer reloads the page.
