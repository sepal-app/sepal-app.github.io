- Sepal records a garden's accessions, the material that stands in its locations, and the taxa they belong to. Taxon names come from the World Flora Online Plant List, with synonyms and distribution.
- An accession records what was received and how much, its intended location, its supplier contact and any wild collection data.
- You can attach media, tags and notes to records, and every change appears in the activity feed.
- Full-text search covers every list, and a query builder adds field filters.
- An owner invites users and gives each one a role, including read-only access.
- Sepal runs as one process with one SQLite database, so you can host it yourself with Docker. A setup wizard creates the first user and downloads the taxon data, and backups run on a schedule.
- This site has docs, a blog and this changelog.
